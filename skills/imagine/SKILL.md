---
name: imagine
description: Imagine and evaluate an implementation or refactoring proposal.
---

The user has given you a proposal for an implmentation, refactoring, or structural change.

Imagine what that would look like in real life.

Present a high-level overview of what needs to be changed.

Then present your evaluation of the proposal.

Consider:
- Will it actually solve the problem?
- What parts will be affected or broken?
- What risks and complications do you foresee?
- Will it improve architecture/readability/maintainability/operability/security/performance? Or will it make it worse?
- Will it make the codebase slimmer or fatter?
- How large would the change be?
- Is it worth it overall?
- If not, what do you propose instead?

Be creative and think outside the box. Take initiative.

It is often not important to preserve every behavior of the original code.
Do not assume that just because the original code did something a certain way that it is the best way to do it.
If major improvements can be had by changing the behavior a bit, consider that as well.
